Sustainable & Recycled Concrete Technologies

Structural concrete research for a lower-carbon built environment.

Assistant Professor of Civil Engineering with a Ph.D. from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhubaneswar and postdoctoral research at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras. My work sits at the intersection of Recycled Aggregate Concrete, Limestone Calcined Clay Cement (LC³), geopolymer systems, and industrial-waste utilization — backed by Life Cycle Assessment and machine-learning-based property prediction.

Selected Impact
3D-Printed Construction

Led the Life Cycle Assessment for India’s first 3D-printed G+1 building with Tvasta Manufacturing — findings fed directly into low-carbon mix optimization and emerging 3DCP sustainability standards in India.

Recycled Aggregate Concrete

Profile & Research Interests

Author of 8 peer-reviewed journal articles (Q1/Q2, SCIE-indexed) on Recycled Aggregate Concrete (RAC), LC³, geopolymer systems, and industrial-waste utilization in concrete. Led the LCA study for India’s first 3D-printed G+1 building at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, in partnership with the deep-tech startup Tvasta Manufacturing. Combines active experimental and computational research with classroom teaching in structural analysis, concrete technology, and structural design, alongside institutional experience in academic quality assurance (IQAC) and digital academic administration.

  • Recycled Aggregate Concrete (RAC) and treated / blended recycled aggregates
  • Limestone Calcined Clay Cement (LC³) and geopolymer concrete systems
  • Industrial & foundry waste utilization — waste foundry sand, GGBS, fly ash, metakaolin
  • Durability & corrosion performance — RCPT, surface resistivity, accelerated carbonation
  • Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of construction materials & 3D-printed concrete
  • Machine-learning-based prediction modeling for concrete mix design & performance

Services

For industry partners: I take a mix, material, or durability problem from lab characterization through to a documented, defensible result. Typical engagements run as a scoped test program or a fixed-deliverable report — get in touch to discuss scope and timeline.

01

Technical Writing

Research papers, technical reports, and grant/project documentation in concrete materials and structural engineering.

Deliverable: a publication-ready or client-ready written report.

02

Concrete Mix Design

Mix design for conventional, recycled-aggregate, and blended-cement concretes, including performance optimization.

Deliverable: a trial-tested mix proportion with target-strength, workability, resistivity verification.

03

Mechanical Testing

Compressive, tensile, and flexural strength characterization using UTM and related laboratory instrumentation.

Deliverable: strength data with test methodology and interpretation.

04

Durability Testing

RCPT, surface resistivity, accelerated carbonation, and corrosion assessment of concrete and reinforced systems.

Deliverable: a durability assessment report against relevant standards.
